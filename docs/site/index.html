THE PLAYER ROUTE

One connected technology tree

Use the Slimefun Guide to search each bold item. Every stage produces the prerequisite for the next; machine input and output slots also accept Slimefun Cargo and Networks accessors.

UNIVERSAL AUTOMATION

Teach an AI what to manufacture

Recipe intelligence

Place a real Slimefun item in the target slot. The AI reads its registered recipe, groups exact Slimefun IDs and vanilla materials, weighs rarity and publishes its J/t draw and powered runtime. Networks or Cargo can continuously supply the 18 reserved material slots.

No-loss transaction

The target is only a template. Materials remain untouched until 100%; target, progress and transaction phase persist across restarts. Completion commits output first and debits materials second, so a JVM crash can never erase an Infinity item. Infinity-class jobs require at least three powered days by default.

OPERATING MANUAL

Powered machines, not decorative heads

How every processor worksPower it through Slimefun Energy → insert the shown prerequisite in the green input → leave output space → click Run Secured Batch. Cargo inserts only into input and withdraws only from output.
MachineInputOutputPower contract

Batches are transactional: the machine atomically charges J/t × rated seconds × 20, consumes the input and commits the output. Rated seconds define energy cost, not a fake visual timer. Every machine item is a PLAYER_HEAD with a validated Minecraft texture payload; no unskinned machine head is registered.

FIELD SYSTEMS

What the equipment actually does

ARC Aegis VI

92% conversion

Reduces incoming damage to 8%, with a visible cyan impact shell. It is powerful, but void conditions and a cooling Zero-Point Anchor still matter.

Nanite Regenesis VI

Material has a cost

Every repair pulse consumes a Nanomass Canister, restores health and repairs all damaged equipped armor. No nanomass means no regeneration.

Zero-Point Anchor VI

30-minute cooldown

Rejects one lethal state and returns the wearer at low health. The cooldown prevents true immortality while approaching Infinity-tier survivability.

Stark Vault Network

54 protected slots

Modular, Nanomass and Armory vaults implement native Slimefun inventory transport. Connect Cargo nodes or a Networks accessor; breaking returns contents safely.

God-Prison Cage

Owner-bound flight

Activate the core inside your own ProtectionStone. Flight is limited to +20/−10 blocks and burns cells according to protected volume every 15 seconds.

Infinity Snap

Mobs only

Players cannot enter its target collection. Protected regions fail closed, no chunks load, no blocks change and every effect has a hard entity budget.

NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY

Power without server damage

  • Large abilities abort near every protected region, including your own.
  • Players are excluded from Snap, beams, shields, cataclysms and tactical weapons.
  • No ability creates a Bukkit block explosion or loads distant chunks.
  • Protection lookup uncertainty denies activation instead of assuming wilderness.
  • Effects, targets and loops have hard budgets to protect TPS and clients.
  • Stable Slimefun/PDC IDs—not names or lore—control all abilities.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before asking staff

Where do I see exact recipes?

Open the Slimefun Guide and search “Drakes Nanotech”. The guide is authoritative for the installed build; this website explains order and operation.

Why will my machine not run?

Check the exact input ID, output space and stored energy. The control item shows input, output, draw and buffer inside the menu.

Why did God-Prison flight stop?

You left your owned ProtectionStone, crossed +20/−10 from activation altitude, ran out of volume-priced power cells or disconnected.

Can Networks store items from Stark Vaults?

Yes. Place a normal Networks inventory accessor against the vault. Cargo access is intentionally input/output-safe and does not require Networks as a hard dependency.
